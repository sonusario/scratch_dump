# ISO/IEC 27001 and 27002 (aggregated)

**Source BoK:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 *Information security management systems — Requirements* (with Amd 1:2024 on climate), and ISO/IEC 27002:2022 *Information security controls*.
**Why aggregated:** both are paid ISO standards. NIST's free documents cover the same ground in more depth, and NIST publishes an official mapping, so this file gives the full structure and points each part to its free NIST equivalent.
**Gov tie:** G3. Not required for US federal systems (NIST is), but NIST maintains official 800-53 ↔ 27001 mappings, FedRAMP accepts ISO 27001 evidence in some places, and many federal contractors certify to it. 27001 was itself influenced by BS 7799, which drew on the same control ideas as US DoD's earlier work.

## 1. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clauses (the management system)

Clauses 4–10 are mandatory requirements (Annex SL harmonized structure, shared with ISO 9001, 22301, 42001).

| Clause | Requirement | Free equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| 4 Context of the organization | Internal/external issues; interested parties and their requirements; ISMS scope; establish the ISMS. | [NIST SP 800-37 Prepare step](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/37/r2/final); CSF 2.0 GV.OC |
| 5 Leadership | Top management commitment; information security policy; roles, responsibilities, authorities. | 800-53 PM family; CSF GV.RR, GV.PO |
| 6 Planning | Actions to address risks and opportunities; **information security risk assessment** and **risk treatment**; **Statement of Applicability (SoA)**; objectives; planning of changes. | [SP 800-30r1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/30/r1/final) (risk assessment); 800-37 Select step (≈ SoA) |
| 7 Support | Resources; competence; awareness; communication; documented information. | 800-53 AT family; NICE framework |
| 8 Operation | Operational planning and control; perform risk assessments and treatment. | 800-37 Implement step |
| 9 Performance evaluation | Monitoring, measurement, analysis; **internal audit**; **management review**. | [SP 800-55 (measures)](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/55/v1/final); [SP 800-137 (ISCM)](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/137/final); 800-53A |
| 10 Improvement | Continual improvement; nonconformity and corrective action. | 800-53 CA-5 POA&M |

**Annex A** lists the 93 controls of 27002 as a reference set; the SoA must say which apply and why.

### Attributes (new in 2022)
Each control is tagged with: control type (preventive/detective/corrective), information security properties (C/I/A), cybersecurity concepts (Identify/Protect/Detect/Respond/Recover, i.e. CSF functions), operational capabilities, and security domains.

## 5. Certification basics

Third-party certification bodies (accredited, in the US, by ANAB) audit in two stages (documentation, then implementation), issue a 3-year certificate, and run annual surveillance audits. Organizations moving from 27001:2013 had until **31 Oct 2025** to transition to the 2022 version.
