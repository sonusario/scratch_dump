# PMBOK: Project Management Body of Knowledge (aggregated)

**Source BoK:** *A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge* (PMBOK Guide), Project Management Institute. 7th edition (2021) is principle-based; the 8th edition (2025) brings principles, performance domains, and processes back together. ANSI/PMI 99-001.
**Why aggregated:** the full text is only free to PMI members. Free government sources (GAO, DAU) cover cost, schedule and EVM in more depth than PMBOK does, but nothing free lays out the whole BoK in one place.
**Gov tie:** G3. Not mandated, but federal program/project manager certification (FAC-P/PM) and DoD's DAU curriculum are built on the same structure, and PMP is accepted toward FAC-P/PM.

## 1. Structure at a glance

PMBOK has had two organizing ideas over its history. Both are still in use, so both are given here.

| View | Editions | Organizing unit |
|---|---|---|
| **Process-based** | 1st–6th (1996–2017), and the *Process Groups: A Practice Guide* (2022) | 5 process groups × 10 knowledge areas = 49 processes, each with inputs, tools & techniques, outputs (ITTOs) |
| **Principle-based** | 7th (2021), 8th (2025) | 12 principles (7th) / 6 principles (8th) + 8 (7th) / 7 (8th) performance domains + tailoring + models, methods, artifacts |

## 2. The process view (49 processes)

### Process groups

1. **Initiating:** define and authorize the project or phase.
2. **Planning:** establish scope, objectives, and the course of action.
3. **Executing:** do the work in the plan.
4. **Monitoring & Controlling:** track, review, regulate; identify and make changes.
5. **Closing:** formally complete the project or phase.

## 3. The principle view (7th / 8th editions)

### 7th edition: 12 principles

1. Be a diligent, respectful, and caring steward.
2. Create a collaborative project team environment.
3. Effectively engage with stakeholders.
4. Focus on value.
5. Recognize, evaluate, and respond to system interactions.
6. Demonstrate leadership behaviors.
7. Tailor based on context.
8. Build quality into processes and deliverables.
9. Navigate complexity.
10. Optimize risk responses.
11. Embrace adaptability and resiliency.
12. Enable change to achieve the envisioned future state.

### 7th edition: 8 performance domains

| Domain | Outcome it aims at |
|---|---|
| Stakeholders | Productive relationships and agreement on objectives. |
| Team | Shared ownership, high performance, servant/leadership. |
| Development Approach & Life Cycle | Predictive, adaptive, or hybrid approach that fits the product, with sensible phases and cadence. |
| Planning | Organized, coordinated, evolving plans. |
| Project Work | Efficient processes, resources, procurement, knowledge management. |
| Delivery | Delivering scope and quality that realize value. |
| Measurement | Reliable metrics (KPIs, EVM, burn charts, forecasts) driving decisions. |
| Uncertainty | Awareness and responses to risk, ambiguity, complexity, volatility. |

### 8th edition (2025)

As described in PMI's announcements (verify details against PMI's site, since this is from secondary summaries):

- **6 principles:** adopt a holistic view; focus on value; embed quality into processes and deliverables; be an accountable leader; integrate sustainability within all project domains; build an empowered culture.
- **7 performance domains:** Governance, Scope, Schedule, Finance, Stakeholders, Resources, Risk.
- **5 focus areas** that play the role of the old process groups: Initiating, Planning, Executing, Monitoring & Controlling, Closing.
- About **40 processes** are back inside the Guide, organized by domain and focus area, so the process and principle views are reunited.
- More material on AI, sustainability, and procurement.

PMI's own free summary pages are the best free source for the current lists: [pmi.org/standards/pmbok](https://www.pmi.org/standards/pmbok).

## 4. Development approaches

| Approach | Use when | Free source |
|---|---|---|
| Predictive (waterfall) | Requirements stable; regulated deliverables. | [DoD SE Guidebook](https://www.cto.mil/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/SE-Guidebook-2022.pdf) |
| Iterative / incremental | Learning or partial value early. | SEBoK [life cycle models](https://sebokwiki.org/) |
| Agile (Scrum, Kanban, XP) | High uncertainty, frequent feedback possible. | [Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/); [GAO Agile Assessment Guide](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-24-105506) |
| Hybrid / scaled | Large programs mixing both. | [SAFe](https://framework.scaledagile.com/); [DoD Software Acquisition Pathway](https://aaf.dau.edu/aaf/software/) |
